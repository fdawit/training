# Training Path 15 — Program Specification

This file records the workbook rules implemented by the app. The app generates seven calendar modules for each of 15 weeks: Monday Workout A, Tuesday Jefferson Curl, Wednesday Workout B, Thursday Jefferson Curl, Friday Workout C, Saturday recovery, and Sunday Field Hour plus Jefferson Curl. Big Three and 10,000 steps appear every day; full mobility appears before Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Sunday sessions.

## Phases

| Phase | Weeks | Purpose | Main-lift RPE cap |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Reacquaint | 1–5 | Restore patterns and let connective tissue catch up to muscle | 7 |
| Rebuild | 6–10 | Return to and pass test-day capacity | 8 |
| Express | 11–15 | New territory on the main lifts | 8.5 |

Deload Weeks 5, 10, and 15 use an RPE cap of 6.

## Main-lift formula

`nearest 5 lb (estimated 1RM × Week-14 target × weekly multiplier)`

Week-14 targets are 80% for Back Squat 3×6, 82% for Conventional Deadlift 3×5, 70% for Romanian Deadlift 3×8, and 75% for Front Squat 2×6. The weekly RPE cap always overrides the number.

## Fixed safeguards

- Miss one session: skip it, continue on schedule, never double up.
- Miss one week: repeat the previous week's loads.
- Miss 2–3 weeks: return to the first week of the current phase.
- Miss 4+ weeks: restart at Phase 1, Week 2.
- More than 14 days since Field Hour: drills and build-ups only; earn bounds and intervals back over two sessions.
- Jefferson Curl: never within two hours of waking, never before lifting, and standalone days require a five-minute warm-up.
- Jefferson load increases only after full range, no hesitation, and maintained control.
- Bulgarian Split Squat: bodyweight Weeks 1–2, light dumbbells Weeks 3–5, normal progression from Week 6.
- Primers stay light; if they feel like working sets, they are too heavy.
